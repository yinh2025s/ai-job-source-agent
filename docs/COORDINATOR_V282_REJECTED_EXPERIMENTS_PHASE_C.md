# Coordinator `.282` Rejected Experiments - Phase C

Date: 2026-07-29

Two isolated Phase B implementations were reviewed and rejected before any
full-cohort measurement.

## GovernmentJobs

The initial rendered keyword fallback completed 3/3 focused records but
recovered zero Exact openings. Wichita and College Station changed from fetch
failure to an unsupported JavaScript shell; Lubbock still failed before a
verified inventory.

The provider's public frontend script revealed its first-party inventory
transport at `/careers/home/index?agency={tenant}&keyword={title}`. Serial
checks confirmed a valid response for all three tenants, but only College
Station contained the target opening. Lubbock returned a different compliance
role, and the Wichita tenant represented the City of Wichita rather than a
continuous employer identity for `WICHITA COMPANY LIMITED`.

The proposal therefore did not provide three common terminal recoveries and
introduced a cross-employer publication risk. It was reverted.

## Career Probe Deadlines

The per-probe deadline and circuit implementation completed the three-record
focused gate, but only Diamondback Energy recovered to Exact. NDIT and ARUP
remained network timeouts after separate homepage, sitemap, blind ATS and
search probe scopes consumed roughly the same outer budget.

One recovery does not satisfy the three-company/three-recovery rule, and the
large scheduler change did not close the claimed cluster. It was reverted.

## Final State

The adapter version returned from `.282` to `.281`. The resulting source tree
is identical to the accepted `.281` measurement commit. No Fresh100, Frozen100
or sealed-cohort aggregate was changed by either experiment.
